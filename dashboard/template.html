Can a model take the first pass?

Two models trained on 80% of complaints and tested on the other 20%. Copies of the same text were kept on one side of the split, so the scores reflect complaints the models had never seen.

    Methods and limits

    How this was built

    Data

      Pipeline

      • Python and pandas clean the text and engineer intake-time features; SQL (SQLite) produces the benchmark tables.
      • Themes: TF-IDF on unique narratives plus non-negative matrix factorization (18 topics merged into 16 themes).
      • Tone: VADER sentiment, scored sentence by sentence.
      • Models: scikit-learn logistic regression, evaluated on a 20% hold-out split grouped by narrative text.

      Read with care

      • Outcomes are reported by the companies themselves, so some gaps reflect how a company labels its responses.
      • Narratives are published only when consumers consent, so this is not every complaint filed.
      • Gaps are associations, not causes. "Expected" adjusts for product mix only.
      • Rates for small slices (marked †, under 100 complaints) are noisy; slices under 30 are hidden.
